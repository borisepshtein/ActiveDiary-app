# ActiveDiary

A Mac diary that interviews you. Every day, at a time you choose, ActiveDiary
asks you a short, specific question about your day and follows up like a
curious friend would — then keeps the conversation as your diary entry.

- **Remembers what matters to you** and asks about it, lightly.
- **Voices**: talk to your diary as Marcus Aurelius, Mark Twain, Sigmund Freud — or anyone you name.
- **Boundaries**: tell it a topic is off-limits and it never brings it up again.
- **Aware of your day**: local weather, your calendar, and plans you mentioned — which it can add to Reminders.
- **Weekly summary** on Sundays, and an on-demand deep analysis of your whole diary.
- **Your diary stays on your Mac.** To write its questions and summaries, ActiveDiary sends conversation text to Anthropic's Claude API, and looks up weather for your city (and any place you mention traveling to) with Open-Meteo. No account, no subscription.

ActiveDiary is not therapy or a mental-health service.
